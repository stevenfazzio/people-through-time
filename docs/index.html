People Through Time

The most famous people of every era, from all Wikipedias, placed by what they are known for. Move the slider below to travel through birth years.

    About this map

    31,000 people with a Wikipedia article in any language, chosen by median monthly views (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026, all Wikipedias) × years since birth. Each slider position shows the ~4,300 people born in five consecutive birth-year bins; each person's place comes from the opening of their Wikipedia article (Qwen3 embeddings, laid out with AlignedUMAP so people glide between windows). Regions were found in each window, followed across windows and named by Claude. Click a person to open their article.

    Text and images: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and Wikidata; leads with no English article were translated by Claude.

    Drag the band or press ← →. Each ribbon is a region of the map, as thick as its number of people.
