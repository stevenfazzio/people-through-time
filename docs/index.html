    About this map

    31,000 people with a Wikipedia article in any language, chosen by median monthly views (Nov 2025 – Oct 2026, all Wikipedias) × years since birth. Each slider position shows the ~4,300 people born in five consecutive birth-year bins; each person's place comes from the opening of their Wikipedia article (Qwen3 embeddings, laid out with AlignedUMAP so people glide between windows). Regions were found in each window, followed across windows and named by Claude. In the chart below the map, each ribbon is a large region's share of the window's people in large regions; the 8–42% of each window in no large region (the grey points) is left out. Click a person to open their article.

    Text and images: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and Wikidata; leads with no English article were translated by Claude.
